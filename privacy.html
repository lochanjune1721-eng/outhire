Privacy

What is stored, and what other people can see. This is a template — have a lawyer read it before you take money.

What is stored

  • Your email address, so you can sign in and hold a balance.
  • A display name, if you set one.
  • Your balance, what you have spent, and every contribution you have made.
  • Payment records from the payment provider. Card details never reach this site.

What other people see

Contributions are public and permanent — that is the point of the site. Fan leaderboards show your display name and totals. Choosing stay anonymous hides your name everywhere it would otherwise appear; the contribution itself still counts and is still public.

Your email address is never shown to anyone. It is not readable through the public API — the database grants access to your handle and totals and to nothing else.

Deletion

Ask and your account and display name will be removed. Contributions already made stay on the boards, detached from your name, because removing them would rewrite other people’s rankings.
