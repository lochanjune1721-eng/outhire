About

Everyone argues about this for free. Here it costs something, so it means something.

GOAT.lol is a set of leaderboards for the greatest of all time, across sport, screen, music, science, business and culture. There is no voting. You buy credit, you spend it on whoever you believe in, and the total is the rank. That is the entire mechanic.

Inside each person’s page is a second leaderboard: their backers, ranked by how much they have spent. The top three are named on the person’s card. Backing someone is public.

Why credit rather than paying per bid

Card fees are a fixed amount plus a percentage, so a $1 payment loses most of itself to the processor. One top-up and then unlimited $1 bids from balance means the fee is paid once, which is what makes a $1 bid possible at all.
