Rules

All of them. There is nothing else running underneath.

Credit

  • Minimum top-up is $5. After that, $1 minimum per bid, whole dollars only.
  • Credit never expires and is non-refundable.
  • Adding a new person to a board costs $1 from your balance, the same as any bid, and that dollar is their first backing.

Ranking

  • Rank is the money. No algorithm, no free votes, no weighting.
  • Money is cumulative. $10 on someone at $40 makes them $50.
  • Taking #1 in a category costs at least $5 more than the current leader.
  • Equal totals stay in the order reached — the earlier one keeps the higher rank.
  • Nothing expires, decays, or is refunded.
  • Anyone can back anyone. Nobody owns a person’s board.
  • Every contribution is public and permanent.
  • Each person has a board of their backers by total spent. The top three are named on their card.

Who can be listed

  • Real public figures with a Wikipedia entry.
  • No private individuals. No listing yourself.
